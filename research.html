The research wing studies one question: how can AI make far more film, in far more versions, without losing what the film has established? We build the answer, measure it, and publish what we find, including when it fails.

What we research

Three programmes.

01 · Film memory

One record of the film

A versioned record of every person, place, prop, look and decision, with story time, shooting order and edit order kept apart. Every tool and vendor answers to it.

In development

02 · Evidence-based continuity

A check that shows its evidence

Every new shot compared with what the film has established, with frames, timecodes and one of four verdicts: confirmed break, explained change, needs review, insufficient evidence.

Prototype

03 · The model ladder

From frontier to your own machines

The same jobs done by one frontier model, by frontier models combined, by open models they teach, and by open source alone. A locked exam decides which one earns each job.

How we are building media at scale

Scale is not more prompts.
It is one memory.

Anyone can generate a thousand shots. The hard part is a thousand shots, in twelve versions and six languages, that all belong to the same film. That is what we are building.

One storyOne record of the film25 workflowsEvery formatEvery languageEvery platform cutEvery shot checkedEvery AI shot traced

One story, every format

The same record drives the one-minute vertical, the short and the feature, so a change made once reaches every version.

Agents that do the work

V AI runs each step as a workflow: it reads the record, does the job with the right tool, checks the result and asks a person to approve it.

A check nobody can skip

Every shot, from any generator or vendor, is checked against the record before it reaches the edit. The one who made it never certifies it.

How a model earns a job

The loop.

01

A locked exam

Cases no model trains on. A changed exam is refused.

02

A scoreboard

Every model sits the same exam, frontier or local, and the score is kept.

03

A handover rule

A small local model takes a job only when it beats the bar.

04

Lessons, with consent

Corrections become lessons only from work whose owners agreed in writing.

How we work

Four rules.

Publish either way

We publish what we measure, whether it flatters us or not.

Measure, never claim

No percentages we have not measured. No model is called right every time.

Consent first

No client footage or student work trains a model without written consent.

Independent check

The system that makes a shot is never the one that certifies it.

Test it on your film

Studios that want to put the check and the workflows through a real reel can work with the research wing directly.
