What failed, and what we will test

The false alarms.

What failed

On a real scene with no breaks, the rules flagged changes that were not breaks, such as a different angle, a person entering a wider shot, or a prop out of view.

Our hypothesis, and the test

We think comparing free-text descriptions is the cause. We will give the record named people, places and props with stable identities, rerun the same scene, and add a scene with a known break. We will publish both results either way.

How we work
