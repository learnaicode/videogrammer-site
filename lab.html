The Lab · continuity research and development

Steering is not measuring.

Approved references steer a generator toward the right image. They do not tell you whether the shot that came back matches the film. The Lab builds the check that does, and measures it in the open.

What the check must get right

Evidence, not suspicion.

What it must never do

  • Treat an unseen hand as a missing hand
  • Treat a reverse angle as a new location
  • Treat a wider shot that reveals someone as a break
  • Treat an AI label, or a "passed" field, as proof

The four verdicts

  • Confirmed break, with the frames and timecodes
  • Explained change, deliberate in the story
  • Needs review, by a person
  • Insufficient evidence, said out loud

We measure three things separately: what the system sees in a frame, whether it judges continuity correctly, and whether the whole run from footage to report holds up.

The loop

How a model earns a job.

01

A locked exam

Cases the models never train on. A changed exam is refused.

02

A scoreboard

Every model sits the same exam, frontier or local, and the score is kept.

03

A handover rule

A small local model takes a job only when it beats the bar on the exam.

04

Lessons, with consent

Corrections become lessons only from work whose owners agreed in writing.
