The film's memory

Five kinds of knowing.

Observed

What V AI actually saw in a frame or read in a file, with where it saw it.

Proposed

What it inferred or suggests. Never treated as fact until someone approves it.

Approved

What the filmmaker decided, by whom and when. Never silently rewritten.

Unknown

What could not be seen or was never given. Said out loud, not guessed.

Changed on purpose

A costume torn in scene four, a scar after the fight. The story moves on; earlier scenes keep the earlier state.

Three clocks

Story time, shooting order and edit order are kept apart. A flashback to before the tear gets the intact jacket. A take keeps the exact reference it was made from, even if the reference changes later.
