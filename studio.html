Pilot by enquiry

Continuity pilot

For
Small hybrid-production teams receiving repeated revisions from several tools or contributors.
You supply
One agreed scene or sequence, its approved references, and the revision when it comes back. File types and length agreed before we start.
Included
An approved reference record for the scene; a person-led review assisted by our experimental check; one review of the returned revision.
You receive
Findings with frames and timecodes, each reviewed by a person; a correction list; a short note on the revision.
Not included
Fixing the shots. More than one revision review. Any guarantee that every break is found.
Scope and timing
Agreed in writing before work starts.
Approvals
You approve the brief, the plan and each delivery. Final cut is yours.
