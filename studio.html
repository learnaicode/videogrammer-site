Three ways to work with us

Make, fix, or build.

Make

Commission a film

A music video, a short, a campaign or a branded film. We take it from the story to the delivery files, with every step planned and recorded by V AI and every creative decision approved by you.

Fix

One reel, two weeks

Bring the reel that worries you, before the grade. We check it for continuity breaks across every vendor and generator, on your machines, and hand back a report and a fix plan. Our check is in early testing, and we say so in the report.

Build

V AI for your studio

The workflows your productions repeat, built for your team: the tools you already use first, then connected, then custom code last. You choose how the AI is built, from one frontier model to fully open source on your own hardware.

How we build the AI for you

Four ways. Your rules.

01

One frontier model

The most capable model available, end to end, so one mind holds the whole film.

02

Frontier models, combined

Each job to the model that does it best, and a reviewer that did not make the shot.

03

Open models, taught by the frontier

Small models trained on your repeated jobs, on your machines, each passing a locked exam first.

04

Fully open source

Open models and tools on your hardware. Nothing leaves the building.

We tell you which tier fits, what each costs to run, and exactly what leaves your machines in each. We never claim a model is right every time.
